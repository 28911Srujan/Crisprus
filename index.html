2-Mark Questions (Short Answer Type) Question 1: What is Sustainable Development?

> Official Answer: > Sustainable development is development that meets the needs of the present generation without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. It balances three core elements: economic growth, environmental protection, and social well-being. >
Question 2: Name any 4 man-made disruptions that cause ecological imbalance.

> Official Answer: > * Deforestation (large-scale clearing of trees) > * Land degradation and Soil Erosion (overuse of chemical fertilizers) > * Overexploitation of Natural Resources (groundwater depletion, overfishing) > * Industrial and Atmospheric Pollution (dumping industrial toxic waste in rivers/air) >
Question 3: State any two advantages of using 'Kulhads' (clay cups) in place of plastic or paper cups.

> Official Answer: > * Environmental Protection: Reduces plastic waste and prevents tree cutting required for paper cups. > * Socio-Economic Benefit: Creates job opportunities and steady income for local potters, boosting the local economy. >
Question 4: What is Organic Farming? State its importance.

> Official Answer: > Organic farming is an eco-friendly agricultural method where crops are grown without using synthetic chemical fertilizers, pesticides, or weedicides. > * Importance: It prevents soil toxicity, maintains natural ecosystem biodiversity, preserves groundwater quality, and produces safe, chemical-free food. >
Question 5: What is 'Upcycling'? How does it help the environment?

> Official Answer: > Upcycling is the practice of taking old, discarded, or unused products/materials and modifying them into new, higher-value products without degrading them down to raw materials. > * Environmental Benefit: It reduces landfill waste, conserves energy, and minimizes raw material extraction. >
